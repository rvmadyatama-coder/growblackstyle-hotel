A Practical Guide to Trading Risk

Build a risk checklist around position size, costs, invalidation points, and the possibility of loss.

Build a sound trading-research process

Trading education should begin with understanding market mechanics and the possibility of loss, not with promises of returns or claims of a guaranteed strategy. A useful process defines the instrument, trading venue, time horizon, position size and conditions that would invalidate an idea.

Core skills to develop

  • Risk sizing: estimate the amount at risk before placing an order and account for gaps and slippage.
  • Costs: include spread, commissions, financing, borrow costs and applicable taxes.
  • Order mechanics: understand market, limit and stop orders and their execution limitations.
  • Leverage and margin: understand maintenance requirements, liquidation and the possibility of rapid losses.
  • Record keeping: document the thesis, entry, exit, costs, outcome and lessons without cherry-picking results.

Evaluate educational claims

Ask whether results are hypothetical or live, whether costs are included, what period and sample were used, and whether losses and drawdowns are disclosed. Backtests can be overfit and past results do not predict future performance. These materials are for general education, not personalized financial advice.
