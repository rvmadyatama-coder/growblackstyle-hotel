Researching the gold market

Gold is traded in global markets and quoted in different currencies and units. A dollar-denominated spot price is not necessarily the retail price paid locally: exchange rates, product premiums, taxes, fabrication costs and dealer spreads can change the final amount.

Factors to compare

  • Real yields: specify the maturity and whether the measure is market-based or inflation-adjusted.
  • Currency movements: identify the dollar measure and the local currency conversion.
  • Demand: distinguish jewellery, technology, bars and coins, exchange-traded products and central-bank activity.
  • Market positioning: futures positioning and liquidity can influence short-term volatility.

Read data with care

Demand reports may be quarterly or monthly while prices update continuously. Do not compare unlike frequencies or infer causation from a simple correlation. Record the source, unit, date and methodology for every figure. Gold prices can fall and gold does not guarantee protection against inflation or losses.
