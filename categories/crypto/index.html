Understanding crypto-asset markets

Crypto-assets trade across multiple venues and networks, and the same asset may show different prices, liquidity and trading conditions across platforms. A quoted price does not guarantee that an order can be executed at that price or that an asset can be withdrawn when needed.

Risks readers should understand

  • Volatility and liquidity: large price gaps, thin order books and wide spreads can increase execution costs.
  • Custody and operational risk: wallet security, private-key loss, platform failure and withdrawal restrictions matter.
  • Leverage: liquidation mechanisms can close positions rapidly and losses may exceed expectations.
  • Technology and governance: smart-contract bugs, protocol changes, concentration and network outages can affect users.
  • Regulation: legal treatment differs by jurisdiction and can change; readers should consult their local regulator.

Check before relying on a market statistic

Identify the venue, quote currency, timestamp, methodology and whether volume is independently verified. Do not assume that a token's market capitalization represents cash available to sell. Crypto-assets are high risk and may not have the protections associated with regulated deposits or securities.
